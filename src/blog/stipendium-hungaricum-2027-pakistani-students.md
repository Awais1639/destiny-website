**Stipendium Hungaricum 2027** applications have not opened yet. When we checked the official website on 23 November 2026, it still showed the 2026/27 calls and no 2027/28 announcement. Last year the online system opened on **17 November 2025**, so the new call could be published any day. This post explains what we know for certain, what is still unconfirmed, and what Pakistani students can prepare right now so they are ready the day it opens.

## Where things stand today

The Stipendium Hungaricum programme is run by the Tempus Public Foundation (TPF) for the Government of Hungary. It funds bachelor’s, master’s, one-tier master’s (for example medicine) and PhD study at Hungarian universities.

| What | Status on 23 November 2026 |
| --- | --- |
| 2027/28 Call for Applications | Not published yet |
| Online application system for 2027/28 | Not confirmed as open |
| Previous cycle (2026/27) opened | 17 November 2025 |
| Previous cycle (2026/27) deadline | 15 January 2026, 2 p.m. Central European Time |
| HEC portal deadline in the previous cycle | 15 January 2026, 4 p.m. Pakistan time |

> Do not plan around last year’s deadline. The 2027/28 dates will only be official once the new Call for Applications and HEC’s advertisement are published. We will update our [Stipendium Hungaricum listing](/scholarships/stipendium-hungaricum/) as soon as they are.

Notice one detail from last year: HEC’s deadline (4 p.m. in Pakistan) came **two hours before** the Hungarian deadline (2 p.m. in Hungary, which is 6 p.m. in Pakistan). Students who aimed only for the Hungarian deadline risked missing the HEC one.

## What the scholarship covered in 2026/27

According to the official 2026/27 Call for Applications for bachelor’s, master’s and one-tier master’s programmes, the scholarship included:

- **Tuition-free study** at the host university.
- **A monthly stipend of HUF 43,700** (about EUR 110), paid 12 months a year until the end of studies.
- **A free dormitory place** (subject to capacity) **or a housing contribution of HUF 40,000 a month** (about EUR 100).
- **Medical care** under Hungarian law, plus supplementary insurance of up to HUF 65,000 a year.

The call itself states that these amounts only partly cover living costs, so you will need some money of your own. Doctoral scholarship amounts are set in the separate PhD call. The 2027/28 amounts may change, so treat these figures as last year’s reference, not a promise.

## The two-portal rule for Pakistani applicants

Pakistan is one of the programme’s Sending Partners, and HEC handles all nominations from Pakistan. In the 2026-27 cycle, HEC made it mandatory to apply on **both** portals:

1. The Stipendium Hungaricum application system: [apply.stipendiumhungaricum.hu](https://apply.stipendiumhungaricum.hu/)
2. The HEC scholarship portal: [scholarship.hec.gov.pk](https://scholarship.hec.gov.pk/) (choose “Hungarian Scholarship Program”)

If you submit on only one of them, your application is not considered. The Hungarian call is equally clear: an application is only considered if the Sending Partner nominates you. A saved but unsubmitted application on the Hungarian portal also does not count.

## HEC eligibility rules from the 2026-27 cycle

HEC’s eligibility page for 2026-27 listed these key conditions. Expect similar rules for 2027-28, but confirm them in the new advertisement:

- You must be a citizen of Pakistan or AJ&K; dual nationals were not eligible.
- There was no upper age limit, but applicants born after 31 August 2008 were not eligible.
- **HAT or USAT with at least 50%.** A HAT result had to be dated on or after 15 January 2024 (valid two years) and a USAT result on or after 15 January 2025 (valid one year).
- Applications were accepted only in the **specified disciplines** listed by HEC.
- Some Hungarian universities ask for IELTS or TOEFL; most accept an English proficiency certificate from your college or university.
- Successful candidates sign a bond to return to Pakistan after the degree.
- For professional degrees (for example engineering or medicine) you are responsible for checking recognition with the relevant council, such as PEC or PMDC.

## What you can prepare this week

Last year the window from opening to deadline was under nine weeks. That is not much time if you start from zero. These steps do not depend on the new dates:

- **Book your HAT or USAT now** if you do not have a valid result. Registration is on [etc.hec.gov.pk](https://etc.hec.gov.pk/). Test dates fill up, and a result that expires before the deadline will not count.
- **Shortlist programmes.** Read the list of available programmes on the official site, check it against HEC’s specified disciplines, and compare cities and living costs on our [Hungary destination page](/countries/hungary/).
- **Draft your motivation letter** around one or two specific programmes. Our guide on [how to write a motivation letter](/guides/how-to-write-a-motivation-letter/) walks you through the structure.
- **Ask for recommendation letters early.** Teachers are busy in December; see [how to request recommendation letters](/guides/how-to-request-recommendation-letters/).
- **Collect transcripts and your English certificate**, and scan everything clearly as PDF. Use our free [document checklists](/tools/checklists/) to keep track.
- **Check your passport or CNIC.** HEC’s FAQ for 2026-27 allowed a CNIC at first, with the passport required after provisional selection.

## What happens after you apply

The 2026/27 timeline on the official site gives a good idea of the rhythm: nominations by Sending Partners by 28 February, the universities’ own admission procedures until late May, results by 30 June, the visa process from mid-August, and arrival in Hungary by the end of September.

## Our take

Stipendium Hungaricum remains one of the most accessible government scholarships for Pakistani students, because Pakistan has its own nomination route through HEC. The biggest mistakes we see are missing the HEC portal, an expired HAT or USAT result, and choosing a programme outside HEC’s specified disciplines. Prepare those three things now, and you will be ready to apply in the first week.
