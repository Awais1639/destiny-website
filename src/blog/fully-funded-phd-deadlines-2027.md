Several **fully funded PhD 2027** calls close in the next ten weeks, and four of them are confirmed on their official pages today: **Stanford Computer Science (8 December 2026)**, **UCC Futures Scholars in Ireland (11 December 2026)**, **ISTA in Austria (8 January 2027)** and **KAIST in South Korea (7 January – 5 February 2027)**. All four accept international applicants. If you want to start a PhD in autumn 2027, the December deadlines are the ones to act on this week.

## Key facts: four fully funded PhD deadlines for 2027 entry

| Programme | Deadline | Funding (as stated officially) | Status today |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Stanford CS PhD (USA) | 8 December 2026 | All PhD students funded while making satisfactory progress | Open since 15 Sept 2026 |
| UCC Futures Scholars (Ireland) | 11 December 2026, 17:00 Irish time | €25,000 a year stipend, up to €5,750 a year towards fees | Open since 2 Nov 2026 |
| ISTA PhD Program (Austria) | 8 January 2027, 3 p.m. CET | Employed PhD; €3,053–3,673 gross a month (2026 rates), 14 payments a year | Open |
| KAIST PhD (South Korea) | 7 Jan – 5 Feb 2027 | KAIST Scholarship (select it in the application) | Opens 7 Jan 2027 |

We checked every date above on the provider's own website on 25 November 2026. Deadlines can change, so open the official page before you submit.

## Stanford Computer Science PhD: deadline 8 December 2026

Stanford's [graduate application deadlines page](https://www.cs.stanford.edu/admissions-graduate-application-deadlines) lists the PhD application for autumn entry as opening on 15 September 2026 and closing on **8 December 2026**, with decisions in February 2027. The page is firm: deadlines are final and late applications are not accepted.

On funding, Stanford CS says all of its PhD students are funded as long as they make satisfactory academic progress. It does not list a stipend figure on that page, and it adjusts your package if you bring external funding.

Be realistic: this is one of the most competitive PhD programmes in the world. Apply only if your research experience and recommendation letters are genuinely strong, and treat it as one option in a wider list. See our [Stanford CS PhD listing](/phd/stanford-cs-phd-funding/) for the details we track.

## UCC Futures Scholars, Ireland: deadline 11 December 2026

University College Cork's [UCC Futures Scholars call](https://www.ucc.ie/en/media/studyatucc/doctoralstudies/Call-Document-UCC-Futures-Scholars-GoI.pdf) opened on 2 November 2026 and closes at **17:00 Irish time on 11 December 2026**. Awards start on 1 October 2027.

What the call offers:

- an annual stipend of **€25,000**;
- a contribution towards tuition fees of **up to €5,750 a year**;
- at least €5,000 a year for research costs.

The call states that applicants of all nationalities are eligible. You need a first-class or upper second-class honours degree or a master's, and you must meet UCC's English requirements.

> The most important rule: you cannot apply alone. The application is submitted through UCC's system by a **lead supervisor** who agrees to support you. With about two weeks left, email potential supervisors today with a short research idea and your CV.

Our [UCC Futures Scholars listing](/phd/ucc-futures-scholars-programme/) and the [Ireland country page](/countries/ireland/) have more background.

## ISTA PhD Program, Austria: deadline 8 January 2027

The Institute of Science and Technology Austria (ISTA), near Vienna, runs one interdisciplinary PhD programme in the natural and formal sciences. Its [application page](https://phd.ista.ac.at/phd-application-admission/) says the 2027 call is open and the deadline is **8 January 2027 at 3:00 p.m. CET**. Your referees must upload their letters by **12 January 2027, 11:59 p.m. CET**, and you can change referee details only until the main deadline.

ISTA's [funding page](https://phd.ista.ac.at/funding-and-awards/) says PhD students are employed by the institute, with 2026 gross salaries from **€3,053 to €3,673 a month, paid 14 times a year**, depending on whether you hold a bachelor's or a master's degree. ISTA charges no tuition and no application fee. The programme starts on 15 September each year.

A useful point for Pakistani applicants: ISTA accepts students with a bachelor's degree, so you do not need a master's first. Check our [ISTA listing](/phd/ista-phd-program/) and the [Austria page](/countries/austria/).

## KAIST PhD, South Korea: applications 7 January – 5 February 2027

KAIST's [admission timeline](https://admission.kaist.ac.kr/intl-graduate/Admission/YearlyTimelines) lists the international graduate application window for **Fall 2027 entry as 7 January – 5 February 2027**. This window is not open yet, so use December to prepare.

To be considered for the KAIST Scholarship, you choose it as your financial resource in the application form. Our [KAIST listing](/phd/kaist-international-graduate-admission-phd/) summarises what the scholarship covers. If you are also thinking about the Global Korea Scholarship, note that KAIST says the GKS 2027 University Track and Embassy Track details will be announced in February. See the [South Korea page](/countries/south-korea/) for other routes.

## What to do now

- **This week:** decide whether Stanford (8 Dec) or UCC (11 Dec) fits your profile. For UCC, contact supervisors immediately.
- **By 1 December:** ask your referees for letters. Give them your CV, transcript and the exact deadline. Our guide on [how to request recommendation letters](/guides/how-to-request-recommendation-letters/) has a template.
- **Prepare documents early:** degree certificates, transcripts, passport and English test results. Use our [document checklists](/tools/checklists/) and the [grade converter](/tools/grade-converter/) to present your CGPA clearly.
- **Write a research statement** that names specific labs or supervisors at each institution. Generic statements are easy to spot.
- **Plan for KAIST:** have everything ready by early January so you can submit soon after the window opens.
- **Browse more options** in our [funded PhD catalogue](/phd/), which shows each programme's status and last-checked date.

## Our take

For students from Pakistan and South Asia, ISTA and UCC are the most practical of these four. ISTA pays a real salary and accepts bachelor's graduates; UCC is open to all nationalities but depends on a supervisor saying yes, which takes time, so start today.

Attest your degree and transcripts through HEC early, because universities sometimes ask for verified copies at the offer stage and attestation queues can be slow in December. Check that your IELTS or TOEFL result will still be valid on the deadline date. Keep a close eye on time zones too: 3 p.m. CET is 7 p.m. in Pakistan, and 17:00 Irish time is 10 p.m.

Finally, a PhD offer depends on your record, research fit and references. No adviser can promise a place. If you would like help shortlisting programmes or reviewing your research statement, our team offers [application assistance](/services/).
