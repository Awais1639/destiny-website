The **Chinese Government Scholarship 2027**-28 is now open to Pakistani students through the Higher Education Commission (HEC), with **61 seats** and a deadline of **Tuesday 5 January 2027 at 4:00 PM Pakistan Standard Time**. The seats are split across 25 bachelor's, 21 master's and 15 PhD places, and you must apply on two portals: HEC's scholarship portal and the official Campus China system.

This is the Type-A route, where HEC nominates candidates to the China Scholarship Council (CSC). It is different from applying straight to a Chinese university under other CSC channels, which HEC does not handle. Below are the facts from HEC's own pages, checked today, and a realistic plan for the next five weeks.

> HEC only counts applications marked **submitted** on its portal. A "saved" application is treated as not received, and an application missing from either portal leads to disqualification.

## Who can apply

HEC's eligibility page sets the following conditions. Ages are measured at the time of application.

- **Nationality:** Pakistani or AJ&K nationals only. Dual nationals are not eligible.
- **Bachelor's:** 12 years of education (intermediate, FA/FSc or A levels) and under 25.
- **Master's:** a 16-year bachelor's degree and under 35.
- **PhD:** a master's degree (MS/MPhil) and under 40.
- **Completed degrees only:** if your degree is not complete by the closing date, you cannot apply.
- **No other scholarship:** you may not hold another scholarship at the same time.
- **Good health,** and you must meet the admission requirements of the Chinese universities you choose.

If you are aiming at a professional degree — medicine, dentistry, engineering, nursing, architecture, law or veterinary science — HEC asks you to obtain accreditation clearance from the relevant Pakistani council (for example PM&DC, PEC or PNC) first. Medical and dental applicants should read the PM&DC notices linked on HEC's programme page before choosing a university, because a degree that is not recognised at home can block your registration later.

## The test requirement: USAT, HAT and CSCA

Every applicant needs a test score of at least **50 out of 100**: USAT for bachelor's applicants and HAT for master's and PhD applicants. HEC lists the score windows it accepts for this scholarship: USAT taken from 6 January 2026 to 25 October 2026, and HAT taken from 6 January 2025 to 25 October 2026.

The page also mentions results of the upcoming USAT and HAT test, "tentatively scheduled on December 13, 2026", while the registration deadline for that test was 22 November 2026. If you do not already hold a valid score, check with HEC's Educational Testing Council (etc.hec.gov.pk) before you rely on a December result.

Bachelor's applicants have one more step: the **CSCA assessment test is mandatory**. HEC asks for a CSCA score report obtained after 1 March 2025, or proof that you have registered for a CSCA exam in November, December or January 2027. Details are on the official CSCA website (csca.cn).

## What the scholarship covers

According to HEC's financial coverage page:

- **Tuition:** fully covered by the host university, including education, administration and health insurance costs.
- **Accommodation:** a free university dormitory place or an accommodation subsidy.
- **Stipend:** a monthly stipend "as per Chinese Government Scholarship policy". HEC does not state the amount, so we do not quote one.
- **Travel:** paid by the student, not the scholarship.

HEC also states that it is only the nominating agency and carries no financial liability for the scholarship. Plan for the cost of your air ticket, medical tests, police certificate and document attestation yourself.

Study length depends on level: four to five years for bachelor's, two to three for master's and three to four for PhD, plus a one- to two-year Chinese language preparatory year where required. Bachelor's students must take the preparatory year unless they hold a valid HSK Level 3 certificate. Master's and PhD programmes exist in both Chinese and English.

## How to apply: two portals, one deadline

1. **HEC portal.** Go to scholarship.hec.gov.pk, update your email and phone number in your profile (HEC uses them after nomination), complete the application under "Learning Opportunities Abroad" and press **Submit**. Keep a signed printout.
2. **Campus China.** Register on the official [Campus China system](https://www.campuschina.org/), choose **Program Category: Type A** and **Agency Number: 5861 (HEC/Pakistan)**, fill in the form, then download and sign it. HEC advises using Firefox or Internet Explorer 11 for this portal.
3. **Do not post anything.** HEC says hard copies are not required at this stage and should not be sent to HEC or the Chinese Embassy.

HEC's programme page lists the documents shortlisted or nominated candidates will need: notarised educational documents, CNIC, domicile and birth certificate (plus IBCC/HEC equivalence where it applies), a study plan or research proposal (at least 200 words for bachelor's, 800 words for master's and PhD), language proof such as IELTS, TOEFL or HSK where applicable, a passport valid until 1 March 2028, the Foreigner Physical Examination Form, a recent police clearance certificate, two recommendation letters for master's and PhD, and an employer NOC if you work. A pre-admission letter from a Chinese university is optional but preferred for master's and PhD applicants. The full list and the official forms are on [HEC's Chinese Government Scholarship page](https://www.hec.gov.pk/english/scholarshipsgrants/lao/CGSP/Pages/default.aspx).

## What to do now

- **This weekend:** read the full HEC advertisement and check your age, degree completion date and test score against the rules above.
- **Check your passport:** it must be valid until 1 March 2028. If it is not, start the renewal now.
- **Shortlist universities:** pick programmes on Campus China that match your subject and the teaching language you can manage. Master's and PhD applicants should start emailing potential supervisors for a pre-admission letter.
- **Write your study plan or research proposal** early — 800 words of focused, original writing takes more than one evening.
- **Ask for two recommendation letters** (master's and PhD) by mid-December, so referees are not rushed over the holidays.
- **Submit on both portals** before the last week of December, and confirm that the HEC status shows "submitted".

## Our take

With 61 seats for the whole of Pakistan, competition will be strong, and the HEC route rewards applicants who meet every formal rule exactly. The most common ways people lose out are simple: a "saved" instead of "submitted" HEC application, a Campus China form under the wrong agency number, or a HAT/USAT score outside the accepted window. Check those three things twice.

Master's and PhD applicants have a real advantage if they arrive with a supervisor's pre-admission letter, so use December for focused emails to professors whose research matches yours. Bachelor's applicants should not overlook the CSCA requirement, which applies only at their level.

Finally, HEC says there is no application fee for this scholarship. Anyone offering a "confirmed" CSC seat for money is not telling the truth — see our guide on [how to identify scholarship scams](/guides/how-to-identify-scholarship-scams/). Consultancy support can help you prepare a stronger file, but selection rests with HEC and the China Scholarship Council, and nobody can promise an award.
